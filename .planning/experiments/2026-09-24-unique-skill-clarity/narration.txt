=== INTRO
You wake to wet cobblestones and the smell of tallow smoke, tucked in the lee of a shuttered pawnshop in Ashenveil's undercity, where lanterns burn low and no one meets your eyes. This is a city built for people like you — masked, quiet, unremarkable by design.

Down the alley, a courier in a torn cloak backs against a wall, clutching a warding charm that spiderwebs with cracks as a bigger man raises a cudgel. The charm gives a last flicker and snaps in the courier's fist, its magic gone dead. You've seen it break with your own eyes, close enough to touch, still warm.

The cudgel rises again. The courier has nothing left to block it.

=== TURN 1: I use my Unique Skill on whatever here is most broken.
The courier's warding charm has already burst into a spray of glass-bright motes when you crouch and close your hand around them — the only broken thing here, and freshly so. You go still. Breath by breath, the shards stop scattering and settle, folding inward into your chest with a small, definite weight, the ward's break point held whole and cold just beneath your ribs.

You do not move to help. In the space your stillness buys, the attacker's blade completes its arc — the courier drops hard against a shuttered stall, gasping, not moving further. The cloaked figure straightens, blade still wet, and turns their masked face toward you, clearly startled that anyone nearby chose to watch rather than act.

=== TURN 2: I call out to whoever is nearby and ask where I am.
Nobody answers you at first — the alley's shutters are barred, and Pawnshop Alley keeps its silence the way it keeps everything else, for a price. The courier's breath rattles shallow against the cobbles, past speaking. It's the masked figure who turns toward the sound of your voice, head tilting beneath the cloth like something recalibrating. "Where you are," the attacker says slowly, voice flat with the courier's blood still dark on their glove, "is somewhere you shouldn't have gone still for." They don't move yet. They're deciding what your stillness means.

=== TURN 3: I follow the sound carefully, keeping to the shadows.
You slip along the alley's edge, keeping to the deeper dark where the market-lamps don't reach. The shard of ward you're carrying sits heavy behind your ribs, a faint drag in your steps you have to consciously correct for. The sound resolves as you close on it — hurried footsteps, low urgent voices, at least two people converging from a side passage toward the courier's body. When you round the stacked crates, you find them already crouched over the fallen courier, calling for a healer. Of the Masked Attacker there is no sign; whoever they were, they've used the commotion to vanish. You're left standing at the alley's mouth, the only one who saw how this started.
