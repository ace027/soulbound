=== INTRO
You wake beneath the slanted floorboards of an Ashenveil grain-house, dust hanging thick in a blade of grey light. The air smells of mildew and old rope. Voices murmur through the boards above — merchants arguing over a shipment gone missing, unaware anyone lies coiled in the crawlspace below, watching a spider mend its web strand by strand in the corner.

Something has followed the sound of your breathing: a rat, thin and shaking, drags itself toward you with a hindleg crushed flat, too broken to flee and too stubborn to stop moving. Footsteps creak closer above. You have seconds before the boards lift and whatever's up there decides what you are.

The ruined rat waits at your hand, and the door above begins to open.

=== TURN 1: I kneel and press my palm to the ground, listening for whatever is moving beneath this place.
You press your palm flat against the packed earth beneath the floorboards, and the world goes quiet in the way it does right before you notice too much. The footsteps above resolve into two distinct treads, uneven — one heavier, dragging slightly, boots that scuff rather than step. Through the dirt you feel nothing living move but the rat, whose pulse flutters thin and fast against your palm like a moth in a jar; whatever wounded it did not come from below.

The footsteps pause directly overhead. A floorboard groans. Someone is standing right above the gap where morning light should be leaking through, and isn't.

=== TURN 2: I call out to whoever is nearby and ask where I am.
You don't move to the shadows — you speak. Your voice cracks up through the gap in the boards, small and raw: "Where am I?" The footsteps above stop dead. A beat of silence, then a low voice, guarded, testing the dark before committing to it: "Who's down there? Show yourself before I call the watch." Boards creak as weight shifts — someone crouching now, peering at the gap, more curious than certain of what they've found. The wounded rat freezes against your palm. Whatever this stranger decides in the next few seconds, you've already given away that you're here.

=== TURN 3: I follow the sound carefully, keeping to the shadows.
You move on hands and knees through the crawlspace, careful to keep your shadow thin against the packed earth, the wounded rat flinching away from your palm as you pass. The footsteps above shift with your movement, unhurried but deliberate, tracking the sound of you rather than losing it. A sliver of lamplight breaks through the gap in the boards ahead, and beneath it a shadow leans down, one hand pressing flat against the wood.

The Grain-House Watcher's voice comes low, close now. "You're not rot-thieves. Rot-thieves don't announce themselves." A board creaks upward an inch, just enough to let more light spill through — an invitation, or a test, you can't yet tell which.
