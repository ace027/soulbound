R12's first bug: legacy line 1348 is `const isMobile = window.innerWidth < 700;` evaluated during
render inside the simulation branch, with no resize listener. Rotating a phone or dragging a window
across 700px leaves the layout stale until something else triggers a re-render.

Keep the breakpoint at `< 700` exactly. `matchMedia('(max-width: 699px)')` is equivalent and gives a
change event; `resize` + `window.innerWidth < 700` is the more literal port. Either is fine — pick
one, and say which and why. Whichever you choose, the initial value must match what legacy computed
on first render, and the listener must be removed on unmount.
