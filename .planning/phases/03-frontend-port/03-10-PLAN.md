(legacy 1022-1027). A stale-closure or dropped-autosave regression is invisible to every other test in
this phase.

listed in "Patterns to Keep", validated twice, and is what surfaced Phase 2's container gaps. Phase 1
was bitten exactly here: "Plan 04 verified non-root against the `runtime` stage; Plan 06 pointed
compose at `builder`; nobody re-checked." Every repo-wide assertion in this phase ran against a
partial tree — plan 03-03's no-Anthropic grep ran when only `lib/` existed; the no-native-dialog grep
never covered `App.tsx`.
