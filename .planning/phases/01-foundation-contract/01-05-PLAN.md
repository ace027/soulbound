<objective>
Move four static data constants out of the legacy artifact into typed modules in the two service workspaces — as a pure copy operation, verified byte-for-byte.
</objective>

<context>
**This is a copy task, not an authoring task.** The prompt text being moved is load-bearing in ways that are not visible from reading it:

- `WORLD_SYSTEM_PROMPT` contains a "MUST NOT" rule list that `CLAUDE.md` constraint #6 describes as load-bearing for game balance. Removals or softenings require explicit developer confirmation. You are not authorized to make any.
- The prompt has been adversarially stress-tested (see `docs/design-decisions-log.md`, "Tier 0 stress tests"). Its resilience is partly a property of its exact wording.
- `WORLD_LORE` holds authored world facts with deliberate unresolved ambiguities — several are documented as needing to *never* resolve. Do not clarify anything.

`CLAUDE.md` constraint #7 requires these stay **separate**: lore facts in `WORLD_LORE`, behavioral/reveal constraints in `WORLD_SYSTEM_PROMPT`. Do not merge them into one module. Do not relocate a rule from one to the other.

Source locations in `legacy/souldbound-world.jsx` (verify these line numbers against the actual file before extracting — read before write):
| Constant | Lines | Destination |
|---|---|---|
| `WORLD_SYSTEM_PROMPT` | 63–216 | `backend/src/data/worldSystemPrompt.ts` |
| `WORLD_LORE` | 219–302 | `backend/src/data/worldLore.ts` |
| `QUESTIONS` | 305–331 | `frontend/src/data/questions.ts` |
| `RACES` | 334–380 | `frontend/src/data/races.ts` |

The two prompts go backend-side because that is where the Anthropic calls will live (Phase 2). `RACES` and `QUESTIONS` go frontend-side because character creation renders them. `TIER_STYLE` is **not** in scope here — it moves with the components in Phase 3.
</context>
