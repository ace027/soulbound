# What's new: playtest build, 16 October 2026

## Start the prologue with a button
The title screen has a new **✦ Begin with the Prologue** button under the usual Begin button. Instead of the five questions, you play a short opening scene, and your soul is read from what you do in it. You no longer need a special link. The usual Begin button still gives you the questionnaire.

## Your character now has stats
- **HP, MP and SP** (health, mana, stamina) show as bars in the Soul Codex. On a phone they sit just above where you type.
- **STR, AGI, INT and WIL** are your attributes. Your race sets where they start.
- **The bars respond to the story.** Fights cost HP, spells cost MP, hard effort costs SP. Rest, healing and meals bring them back. Each change shows in the story log with a short reason, such as "−9 HP: the blade catches your arm".
- **You can't die.** If your HP hits 0, the story continues with a setback: you're captured, you wake somewhere else, you lose something, or someone rescues you.
- **Growth is rare and earned.** A maximum or attribute rises only at a real milestone in the story. Asking for it, grinding, or winning a routine fight won't raise it.
- **Running out of MP or SP doesn't block you.** The story shows you as drained, but you can still try things.

## There's a map
- Open it with the **Map** button next to your location in the Soul Codex, or the **Map** tab on a phone.
- It shows the six kingdoms. Places you've visited are pinned inside their kingdom, and a marker shows where you are now.
- Places whose kingdom isn't known yet sit in an **Uncharted** tray.

## Your old saves still work
Saves from earlier builds load as normal. Old characters get their race's starting stats, and their current location is added to the map as uncharted.

## Known rough edges
- If the narrator describes a place you've already been in different words, it may show up on the map twice.
- The map layout is a sketch of how the kingdoms relate to each other. It isn't geography.
